**Benefits of IEEE: **There are many perks and opportunities that IEEE offers to member students. Some of them are:

## RESOURCES

News, Web TV programs, publications, e-books, and educational resources allow you to stay updated with the latest technologies and trends.

Programs to advance your career goals, increase your skills, give and receive professional mentoring, and much more.

Only IEEE members get exclusive access to the most extensive library of electrical engineering, computer science, and electronics technical literature, as well as technology trends, industry news, and events.

▸ IEEE SPECTRUM MAGAZINE

-Get insights into technological advances and innovation and the dedicated professionals driving continued innovation through this award-winning monthly magazine.

▸ IEEE.TV

-Watch over 2,500 videos covering technology, engineering, author interviews, product news, and conferences.

▸ IEEE XPLORE® DIGITAL LIBRARY

-Get members-only discounted access to nearly 4 million documents spanning journals and magazines, conference proceedings, standards, and more.

▸ IEEE POTENTIALS MAGAZINE

-Learn about career strategies, the latest in research, and how theories

relate to practical applications and critical technical developments and their global impact.

▸ PROCEEDINGS OF THE IEEE

-Access the most highly-cited general interest journal in electrical engineering and computer science.

▸ IEEE ACCESS®

-Examine original research and development results across all fields of interest through this award-winning, multidisciplinary, all-electronic archival journal.

## SCHOLARSHIPS

### Richard E. Merwin Scholarship

IEEE Computer Society is offering $40,000 in student scholarships, from $1,000 and up, to recognize and reward active student volunteer leaders in student branches or chapters who show promise in their academic and professional efforts.

### Upsilon Pi Epsilon Scholarship

The Executive Council of Upsilon Pi Epsilon (UPE) wishes to encourage academic excellence for students in the computing discipline. UPE initiated this award with the IEEE Computer Society, as the largest association of computer professionals, to raise the importance of academic achievement in our future computer professionals.

### Lance Stafford Larson Student Award

In 1983, the Lance Stafford Larson Award was established by the Larson family in memorial for their son, who died in an electrical accident while an undergraduate at the University of Maryland. The Larson family, which includes IEEE Past President Robert Larson, created this award to encourage students to develop excellence in their communication skills and to motivate students toward achievement in the field of Computer Science.

## COMPETITIONS

### IEEE Xtreme Programming Competition

IEEEXtreme is a global challenge in which teams of IEEE Student members – advised and proctored by an IEEE member, and often supported by an IEEE Student Branch – compete in a 24-hour time span against each other to solve a set of programming problems.

### OUTREACH

Achieve More with IEEE Members and the Professional Community

### IEEE COLLABRATEC™

IEEE’s online platform allows members, authors, researchers, and technology professionals with similar fields of interest to network, collaborate, manage content, and create.

### PROFESSIONAL CONNECTIONS NETWORK

Capitalize on a wide range of communications and networking opportunities through IEEE Sections and Chapters, professional conferences, educational seminars, and other local events.

### CONFERENCE CONNECTIONS

Collaborate about the latest research and innovations with those in your area of interest at more than 1,600 IEEE-sponsored conferences and meetings worldwide each year.

### IEEE SOCIETIES

Share your expertise and benefit from hands-on research, journals, conferences, and industry connections within technology-focused societies.

Enhance the value of your membership and connect with like-minded members in many dynamic IEEE special interest groups, such as IEEE Women in Engineering.

### COURSES AND RESEARCH

● Keep learning with an increasing number of IEEE eLearning Library courses, professional certification programs, and other educational opportunities available to IEEE members.

● Expand your personal network with colleagues who share your expertise and commitment.

● Join IEEE Societies or Affinity groups focused on your area of speciality or interest.

## DISCOUNTS

Enjoy discounts on IEEE technical resources, digital libraries, continuing education courses, print and online books, premier conferences, group insurance products, and more.
